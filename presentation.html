Getting Started

Up and running in 4 steps

1
Log in admin sets up your account
Your account has been created. Log in at Moil Primary School's Attendly address with your username and password.
2
Upload attendance data admin only
Export your attendance report from your school's system and upload it. The dashboard updates instantly.
3
Check Today's Actions each morning
Open the dashboard, go to Today's Actions, and work through the checklist. Tick off each student as you contact them.
4
Log contacts and create case plans
Click any student card → log a contact or open Case Management to create a formal attendance case plan.
