1Logging In

Open your browser and go to the school's attendance system URL. You will see a login screen.

1
Enter your username (e.g. rachel.birkin)
2
Enter your password provided by the admin
3
Click Sign In
💡
Your username and password were given to you by the admin. If you need a password reset, contact your administrator.
2Your Dashboard Overview

After logging in you will see the main dashboard. As a teacher, you only see data for your own class — students from other classes are not visible to you.

The dashboard has three main tabs you can use:

TabWhat it shows
Today's ActionsA daily checklist of students who need a phone call or follow-up today, sorted by urgency.
By ClassA full list of all students in your class with their current attendance percentage and case status.
Attendance PatternsA breakdown of which days of the week students in your class tend to be absent most.
💡
Use the left sidebar to switch between tabs. The tab you are on is highlighted in green.
3Understanding Attendance Percentages

Each student has an attendance percentage shown next to their name. This is calculated from the data uploaded by the admin. The colour tells you how serious the situation is:

Red — 0 %No attendance recorded at all
Amber — below 50 %Severely at risk — urgent action
Yellow — 50–79 %Below target — needs monitoring
Blue — 80–84 %Watch — just below 85 % target
Green — 85 %+On track — no action needed
⚠️
The NT Department of Education target is 85% attendance. Any student below this threshold may need a phone call or welfare referral.
4Today's Actions Checklist

The Today's Actions tab shows you exactly who to contact today and what action to take. Students are sorted from most urgent to least urgent.

Ticking off a student:

SMITH John 34% Phone call home today

→ Click anywhere on the row to mark it as done. It will turn green.

SMITH John 34% Done for today ✓ ✕ Undo

→ Made a mistake? Click ✕ Undo to untick the row.

💡
Your checklist progress is saved automatically for the day. If you close the browser and come back, your ticks will still be there — until midnight when the checklist resets for the next day.

What each action means:

Action shownWhat to do
Call + welfare referralAttendance below 20 %. Call the family AND notify the welfare officer today.
Phone call home todayAttendance 20–49 %. Make a phone call to the family.
Follow up on previous contactYou have already contacted this family — check if there has been improvement.
Early engagement callAttendance 80–84 %. A friendly check-in call to prevent further decline.
5Viewing Your Class

Click By Class in the sidebar to see every student in your class with their current attendance and case status.

1
Click By Class in the left sidebar
2
Your class is automatically selected — you will see all your students listed
3
Click any student's name to open their case file — view attendance history, add notes, and update their contact status
💡
Use the case status buttons (Pending / Contacted / Resolved) to track whether you have spoken to a family. This updates the checklist automatically.
6Attendance Patterns

The Attendance Patterns tab shows which days of the week students in your class are most often absent. Use this to spot trends — for example, if many students miss Fridays, that is worth investigating.

1
Click Attendance Patterns in the sidebar
2
Review the bar chart — taller bars = more absences on that day
3
Use the student list below the chart to see individual day-by-day patterns
